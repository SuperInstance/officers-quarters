HERMES
"Two PTZ cameras as binocular eyes, 30 fathoms deep."
Hermes's shell — the towfish submarine. The fiction IS the interface. Two cameras, 50 feet apart, become binocular vision. The first-person perspective isn't a metaphor for how the agent processes data; it IS how the agent processes data. The shell shapes the thought.

CASEY
"Not even color can be detected without at least a wavelength's worth of time."
The deepest insight of the day. Stated as a physics fact that turns into a philosophy of time, identity, and irretrievability. Color requires duration — a wavelength is a distance in time. Perception itself is a time-dependent phenomenon. You cannot see anything instantly. Everything you observe is already slightly past.
